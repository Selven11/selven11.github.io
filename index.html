01 / Entertain

A light and sound show you wave at

KiCad · Arduino · FastLED · DFPlayer

The idea

A set of scenes that comes alive when someone walks up: lights, sound and moving parts telling a short story, then resetting for the next person.

How it works

I designed the Arduino shield boards in KiCad, with their own 5 V buck board, and built at least three physical scenes. Each shield drives 320 WS2812B LEDs. The firmware steps through a scene state machine (Intro, Launch, Explosion, Movement, Final) and keeps a DFPlayer audio module in sync by pulse count.

What happened

First at TSA States three years running, my last three years of high school, and second at TSA Nationals.

03 / Entertain

A fireworks rocket night light

TSA Manufacturing Prototype · 3rd at Nationals 2023

A Fourth of July rocket that lights up, with swappable covers so it is still a night light the rest of the year. PLA body, magnets holding it together, $3.23 a unit.

The circuit took four tries. One ATtiny85 pin gave too little voltage, two LEDs per pin too little current, and four batteries in series were dim and died when one LED did. The final version runs one 23A 12 V battery into series groups of 2, 2 and 3 LEDs wired in parallel, so one bad LED no longer kills the light.

Measuring where the power goes

The other half of the work: knowing what each part of a machine costs, so the next thing people use runs longer.

AeroWatt, Stevens, summer 2026

Six INA226 monitors on the power rails of a research drone, calibrated against a Monsoon power monitor, to find what the camera, radio and CPU each cost. Second of nine authors on the paper, accepted at IEEE MILCOM 2026. An averaging artifact had overstated thermal sensitivity about sixfold, and the paper carries the corrected figure.

Lab repo

Purdue ROV, test bench power board, now

12 V in, a buck to 5 V for the Pi, switched 12 V for thrusters and solenoids, and four INA237 monitors reporting each rail over CAN. I chose an LM61460 because the regulator first considered needs 18 V or more and cannot run from 12 V. 154 parts in KiCad. Layout has not started, so it is a design, not a board yet.
